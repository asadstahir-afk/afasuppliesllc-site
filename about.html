Our story

Over 15 years of getting people what they need.

AFA Supplies LLC is a Texas company that opened its doors in 2010 with a simple idea: buying should not be a second job. Today we help businesses stock their offices, shops and plants, and help homeowners furnish their rooms, with the same promise to both. One person to talk to, one clear price, and no loose ends.

We are small enough that a real person reads and answers your request, and experienced enough to know where to look when the part number is strange, the item has been discontinued or the deadline was yesterday. Every list gets the same care, whether it is one line or one hundred.

Our goal is simple: you tell us what you need, we handle the hunting, haggling and hauling, and you get exactly what you asked for.

What we are good at

The jobs other people give up on.

  1. Finding the hard-to-findOdd part numbers, discontinued items and one-off requests are the work we like best. We search across vendors until we find the right one.
  2. Printing and imaging suppliesOEM toner, ink and label stock matched to the exact model and part number, so what arrives fits what you own.
  3. Fair prices, in writingWe compare vendors and send one written quote with every item and price. You approve before anything is ordered.
  4. Checking before deliveryItems are checked against your order. If something is off, we sort it out with the vendor and tell you what happens next.
