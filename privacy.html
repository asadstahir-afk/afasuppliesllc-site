Privacy

What we do with what you send.

Short version: we use what you send us to answer your request, and nothing else.

What we collect

When you use a request form on this site, we receive what you type: your name, your business if you give one, your email address, a city and state, when you need it, and your list or room description.

How it reaches us

Form submissions are delivered to us by Formspree (formspree.io), a form-handling service that processes them for us. Your message is also visible to the people at AFA who answer requests.

How we use it

To reply to you, prepare a quote, and fulfill orders you approve. We do not sell your information.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which, like any site you visit, can see your IP address when your browser requests them.

Your choices

To ask what we have or to have it deleted, email sales@afasuppliesllc.com.
